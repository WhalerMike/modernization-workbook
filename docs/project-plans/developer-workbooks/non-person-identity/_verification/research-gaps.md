# Research gaps for the developer workbook *Building Non-Person Identity*

Each chapter has its own block of row numbers, so chapters written in parallel don't collide: Chapter NN uses rows (NN − 1) × 10 + 1 to NN × 10 − 1. Chapter 01 has rows 1 to 9, Chapter 02 rows 11 to 19, Chapter 03 rows 21 to 29, Chapter 04 rows 31 to 39, Chapter 05 rows 41 to 49, Chapter 06 rows 51 to 59, Chapter 07 rows 61 to 69, Chapter 08 rows 71 to 79, Chapter 09 rows 81 to 89 and Chapter 10 rows 91 to 99. Unused numbers stay unused; the numbers never change, because the chapters and their logs cite them. Each row was researched on Microsoft Learn (MCP tools) or in the repository on October 30, 2026. Where a later chapter depends on one, the outline in `index.md` says so.
