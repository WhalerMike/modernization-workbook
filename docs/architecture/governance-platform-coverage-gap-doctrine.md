# Governance Platform Coverage and Gap Doctrine

Model for determining what a governance platform covers and what remains a gap.

## Purpose

Not every control or process is delivered by a single platform. This document defines how to classify coverage and how to treat gaps so that residual risk is explicit.

## Coverage classification

| Classification | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| Covered | The platform enforces the control and produces evidence |
| Partial | The platform supports the control but evidence or enforcement is incomplete |
| Gap | The control is required but not delivered by the platform |
| Out of scope | The control is not required for this boundary or workload |

## Gap treatment

1. Record the gap in the Gap Closure Register.
2. Assign an owner and a residual risk rating.
3. Decide: close by configuration, close by compensating control, or accept with approval.
4. Define the evidence that will prove the decision was executed.
5. Review on the continuous monitoring cadence.

## Bottom line

Coverage is not binary. Explicit classification and gap treatment prevent silent residual risk.
