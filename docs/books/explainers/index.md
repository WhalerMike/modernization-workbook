# Explainers {.unnumbered}

An explainer sets out one concept in plain language: what it is, why it matters and how it touches the books. It plays the part of a concept article on Microsoft Learn. Explainers sit beside the books rather than inside them, and each one links to the chapters that carry the design.

Each page keeps its status label (draft or in review) until it is reviewed and proved; see [Status labels](../../about/sources-and-verification.md#status-labels).
