# The Workbook Books {.unnumbered}

The workbook grew as separate whitepapers, guides, series and case studies. The books reorganize that material by topic. Each book opens with one question (its **Why**), then covers **What** changes, **Who** owns it, and **How** to do it. Every external claim is checked against a primary source and endnoted, diagrams follow one house style, and each book has a Word edition and a 15-minute narrated presentation. This site publishes the books and their companions. When a book is built, the source pages it replaces move to the repository's `archive/` folder and their old addresses forward to the new chapters. All twenty topic books are now published; the archived sources stay in the [repository](https://github.com/WhalerMike/modernization-workbook). Book 19, [Building the Attribute-Driven Migration](b19-developer-workbook/index.md), is different: it is a developer workbook, a code-first companion to the Attribute-Driven Migration for the developers who build its middleware, checks and pipelines, reviewed and approved page by page. The developer workbooks for other projects are under [Projects](../project-plans/developer-workbooks/index.md). Each book's deck is on the [Presentations](../presentations/index.md) page.

Baseline is not a claim of independent peer review. A book with a page still waiting for re-approval reads Draft ([Status labels](../about/sources-and-verification.md#status-labels)).
