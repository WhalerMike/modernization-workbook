# Charter Sign-Off Sheet {.unnumbered}

The [program charter](../b00-orientation/03-the-charter.md#governance-and-ownership) fixes the shape of the program's governance and leaves the names blank. This sheet is where the names go. Bring it with [The One-Page Ask](one-page-ask.md).

| Download | Use |
|---|---|
| [Charter_Sign_Off_Sheet.docx](files/Charter_Sign_Off_Sheet.docx) | The form below, one page, ready to fill in |

::: {.callout-note}
## Design guidance
The roles and gate authorities come from [Book 00, Chapter 03](../b00-orientation/03-the-charter.md#governance-and-ownership), [Book 01, Chapter 09](../b01-transformation-arc/09-who-leads-the-transformation.md#decision-rights-across-the-program) and [Book 01, Chapter 12](../b01-transformation-arc/12-migration-roadmap.md). The sheet is a summary, so where it's shorter than those chapters, they govern. Adapt it to your governance structure.
:::

## How to fill it in

1. **Start with three names:** the sponsor, the authorizing official and the steward.
2. **Keep one decider per row.** The one deliberate exception is Gate G2, which the steward and the executive sponsor co-sign.
3. **Build from roles you already have.** OMB M-22-09 required a zero trust strategy implementation lead, and OMB M-19-17 requires an agency-wide ICAM governance structure ([Book 00, Chapter 03](../b00-orientation/03-the-charter.md#governance-and-ownership)). Those people may already belong in these rows.
4. **Record gate decisions where the program records them.** This sheet names who decides. The pull request that merges a gate decision records who made it.

[Book 00, Chapter 07](../b00-orientation/07-who-runs-the-program.md) has the full decision matrix and the workstream RACI.
