## The brief

<!-- form-start -->
**The point:** you're the only official who can accept security and privacy risk on the agency's behalf. The program brings you every risk it carries, in writing, with an end date. In return, you get evidence produced by the work itself, in place of attestation assembled before each assessment.

**What you hold**

- Authorization of systems and boundary changes, and acceptance of residual risk.
- Approval of plans of action and milestones.
- Legacy authentication and NTLM end dates, because keeping legacy authentication open is a risk acceptance and BOD 25-01 makes blocking it a mandatory baseline.
- Deviations from the mandatory BOD 25-01 baselines, which agencies must explain when they report.

**What you get in return**

| Property | What it means for you |
|:---------|:------------------------|
| Gates from data | Gate criteria are evaluated from data, not executive signature |
| Every change a plan | Every plan produces an evidence packet |
| Packages from the source | Authorization packages regenerate from the source instead of spreadsheets |
| Gated actuation | A person approves a corrective action before it runs, with a dry run before production |
| High-risk changes stay gated | Operations with a high blast radius, such as tenant-wide role changes, stay gated permanently |

**What we ask of you**

- Agree the risk-acceptance rows of the decision matrix before the program starts.
- Put the end date of every exception on your calendar.
- Support the four-week pre-flight. Its assessment is read-only, and it shows you the estate as it really is.
