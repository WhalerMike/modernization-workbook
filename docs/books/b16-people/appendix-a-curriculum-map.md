# Appendix A — The Curriculum Map: Role, Book and Track

*Every role the workbook assigns a decision or a task to has a reading list, a track, an assessment and a refresh date; this appendix puts them on one page.*

::: {.callout-note}
## In this appendix
One table per role family, mapping each role from the role models of [Book 00, Chapter 07](../b00-orientation/07-who-runs-the-program.md), [Book 01, Chapter 09](../b01-transformation-arc/09-who-leads-the-transformation.md), [Book 13, Chapter 09](../b13-governance/09-who-governs.md), [Book 14, Chapter 10](../b14-compliance/10-who-owns-compliance.md) and [Book 15, Chapter 10](../b15-day2-operations/10-who-runs-day-two.md) to the chapters it must read, its track, its assessment and its refresh cadence. The reading lists, assessments and cadences are the program's design. The agency should adjust them to its own role definitions and record the result in the learning management system as the assignment rules described in [Chapter 09](09-building-and-running-the-curriculum.md#what-the-learning-management-system-records).
:::

## How to read the map

**Must read** lists chapters by book and chapter number, linked. Every role also reads [Book 16, Chapter 01](01-people-close-the-gap.md) and takes the orientation in [Chapter 09](09-building-and-running-the-curriculum.md#orientation-education-before-training) before anything else; those aren't repeated in the rows. A chapter in the list is required reading for the role. Other chapters in the same book are useful but not assigned.

**Track** is one of four values:

- **Orientation** — orientation and the listed reading only, for roles that decide on evidence other people produce.
- **Compliance** — the [compliance track](03-the-compliance-track.md), which reads the books as evidence for controls.
- **Implementation** — the [implementation track](04-the-implementation-track.md), which reads them as build instructions, with the labs in [Chapter 06](06-vendor-training-and-labs.md).
- **Both** — the role holds decisions on both sides and takes the core of each track.

**Assessment** names the kind of check from [Chapter 05](05-assessment-and-certification.md) that shows the role is ready, using the readiness measures of [Chapter 10](10-measuring-readiness.md#readiness-per-role). Roles marked *assessed* count as ready only with a passing result, not a completion.

**Refresh** is the program's suggested cadence. Every role is also retrained on assignment to the role, on a significant change to the systems or procedures it uses, and when a chapter on its list changes in a way its module owner judges material, as 5 CFR 930.301(d), AT-2 and AT-3 require training on change ([Chapter 09](09-building-and-running-the-curriculum.md#start-from-the-roadmap-not-the-catalog)). Those triggers apply to every row and aren't repeated.[^aA-change]

## Keeping the map current

The map is a derived artifact. Its source is the set of role chapters it cites. When a role chapter adds, renames or removes a role, or a book adds chapters, the map changes with it through the same currency review that [Chapter 09](09-building-and-running-the-curriculum.md#keeping-the-curriculum-current-as-the-books-change) describes for modules. Book 17, AI Identity Governance, is planned ([workbook index](../index.md)); when it is published, the program expects it to add rows for the people who own and approve AI agent identities, and its chapters join the reading lists of identity engineering, the identity governance officer and the CISO's staff.
