# M-19-17 and FICAM Crosswalk {.unnumbered}

OMB M-19-17, *Enabling Mission Delivery through Improved Identity, Credential, and Access Management* (May 21, 2019), is the federal ICAM policy. It tells agencies to govern identity as one program, to manage the identities of devices and automated technologies as well as people, to use PIV and derived PIV, and to federate.[^m1917] The FICAM Architecture on idmanagement.gov breaks the same territory into services. This page maps both onto the workbook, so an ISSO or assessor can find the chapter and the evidence for each requirement, and see which ones the workbook doesn't reach.

It builds on the [Federal Framework Crosswalk](federal-framework-crosswalk.md), which lists M-19-17 among the sources the books cite, and sits beside the [RMF Map](rmf-map.md).

## How to read the marks

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | The workbook says what the requirement asks, who does it, how, and what evidence shows it done |
| **Partial** | The requirement is treated, but only part of it, or without the evidence |
| **Gap** | No page treats it |
| **Out of scope** | Deliberately outside the workbook, with the reason stated |

Marks come from reading the linked pages, not from keyword counts. The FICAM Architecture is **design guidance**: idmanagement.gov describes it as "a framework for an agency to use in the ICAM program and solution roadmap planning," not as policy.[^arch] M-19-17 is policy: its "shall" items are requirements, and it says agencies must implement SP 800-63 and its successors.[^m1917]

## M-19-17, section by section

M-19-17 has six sections, a list of rescissions and a policy-assistance contact. Section I is context; Sections III to V carry the agency requirements; Section VI assigns government-wide work to the standard-setting and shared-service agencies, not to every agency.[^m1917] The section structure was read from the White House PDF on November 2, 2026.

## Gaps, in one list

- **M-19-17 §III.3, cross-government federation**, in part: [Book 07, Ch 09](../books/b07-pki/09-federal-workforce-credentials.md) covers accepting another agency's PIV and federating with agencies and partners, but no page drafts the federation agreement or walks through electronic verification of another agency's PIV assertions.
- **§III.4, continuous vetting** aligned to the FICAM Architecture and CDM.
- **§III.2, PIV for physical access** (PACS, the ISC standard, SP 800-116).
- **§IV Acquisition 2 to 4**: the Approved Products List, Best in Class vehicles for certificates, and CDM as the route for ICAM tools.
- **§IV Governance 2 to 4, in part**: a written ICAM policy, ICAM objectives in the strategic plan, and the update of legacy e-authentication risk assessments.
- **FICAM Federation: Attribute Exchange**, and Policy Alignment in part (Book 07, Ch 09 names the agreements, but the workbook ships no template).
