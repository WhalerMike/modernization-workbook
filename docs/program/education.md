# Education Program

**Building the mental model before teaching the tools**

Before you can use a governed modernization approach, you have to see what it is. That is what the education program does. It is distinct from training: **training teaches how to use and contribute; education builds the conceptual mental model that makes the training stick.**

## The learning arc

The program is not a feature tour. It builds a mental model in a specific order.

1. Governance is a **system**, not a report or a checklist.
2. **Identity** is the primitive. Everything else attaches to a canonical object.
3. **Maps** replace inventories. Adapters produce one unified environment map, not a pile of spreadsheets.
4. **Provenance** is non-negotiable. Every derived artifact names its canonical source and version.
5. **Modernization** is a lifecycle, not a project. It runs end-to-end under change control.
6. **Drift** is the feedback signal. The system knows when reality has diverged from the authoritative source and reports it in a classified, prioritized form.

A learner who internalizes those six ideas is ready to enter training and pick an operator or contributor track.

## Formats

- **90-minute facilitated walkthrough** for program kickoff. One concept per ~10 minutes, with questions between.
- **Self-serve written path** for anyone who cannot attend live. Sufficient on its own.
- **45-minute auditor briefing** — provenance, drift detection, and drift remediation only.
