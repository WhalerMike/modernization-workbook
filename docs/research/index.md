# Research {.unnumbered}

The workbook books are reviewed guidance. This area is where new work starts: an analysis, a product deep dive or a presentation is built here, in the open, and reviewed before its findings move into a book. Nothing here is part of the books until it is promoted.

::: {.callout-note}
## How research is labeled
- **Draft:** being written. Expect gaps and changes.
- **In review:** complete and open for comment. Its claims are sourced, but its conclusions may still change.
- **Promoted:** reviewed, and its findings now live in a workbook book or on the [Presentations](../presentations/index.md) page. The research page stays for its sources and history.
:::

Research follows the same rules as the books: every external claim is checked against a primary source and noted, no specific agency is named, and the site is independent analysis, not an official government publication. Where a research item recommends a product, that recommendation is the program's own analysis, stated with the evidence for and against it. It is not an endorsement by any agency, and the workbook books themselves stay vendor-neutral.

Comments and corrections are welcome as [issues on GitHub](https://github.com/WhalerMike/modernization-workbook/issues).
