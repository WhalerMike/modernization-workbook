# Start Here {.unnumbered}

New to the workbook? Start with this six-minute briefing. It makes one case: modernization should begin with identity, and the first step is small, bounded and can start now. It is written for the people who decide: the executive sponsor, the CIO, the CISO, the chief human capital officer and the authorizing official.

## The case in brief

1. **The clocks nobody can move.** Federal directives and vendor end-of-support dates are set outside the agency; Windows Server 2016 reaches end of support on January 12, 2027 ([Book 00, Chapter 01](books/b00-orientation/01-why-modernize-now.md)).
2. **Everything decides on identity.** Access, devices, data and certificates all decide on who or what is asking, so identity has to be authoritative first ([Book 00, Chapter 01](books/b00-orientation/01-why-modernize-now.md); [Book 01, Chapter 02](books/b01-transformation-arc/02-the-problem.md)).
3. **HR is being rebuilt anyway.** Federal HR 2.0 moves agencies onto one Core HCM platform through FY 2027; OPM rebuilds the HR side, and the identity connection is the agency's ([Book 03, Chapter 03](books/b03-hr-identity/03-federal-hr-2-0.md)).
4. **The access risk is already on the books.** Account decisions live in tickets and spreadsheets, and quarterly reviews can't see a transition when it happens ([Book 03, Chapter 02](books/b03-hr-identity/02-why-hr-is-the-source-of-authority.md)).

**The ask:** approve Phase 0, a four-week pre-flight that needs no vendor decision and produces an inventory, an owner and a date. **The finish line:** Gate G1, Foundation Complete, about four weeks later, measured from data ([Book 01, Chapter 12](books/b01-transformation-arc/12-migration-roadmap.md)).
