# Federal Modernization — Framing Pattern

**End-to-end federal agency modernization arc**

## Starting state

A federal civilian agency carries decades of client-server architecture, Active Directory, on-premises SQL Server, and a traditional perimeter. Modernization has been attempted as a sequence of independent projects. Evidence is assembled retrospectively for authorization.

## Intervention

The program is sequenced as identity-first, then addressing, overlay, telemetry, management automation, and governance at runtime. Governance is in place before any identity object moves. Drift detection and provenance are first-class operational signals.

## Observed behavior (intended)

Mission value is delivered early. Long-tail authorization and drift risk are reduced. Evidence is a byproduct of the migration rather than a retrospective assembly. Phase gates are held on evidence quality and operational determinism, not only on schedule milestones.

## Lessons

- Fund and govern the program as a sequence, not as independent projects.
- Modernization produces the operational conditions required for continuous authorization to be credible.
- Treat the roadmap as a governed delivery arc, not as a claim of full operational completion across every workstream today.
