Two-page leadership brief. States the problem (continuous evidence under FedRAMP 20x), the solution (structured control implementation with evidence byproduct), and the ask (adopt the series as the compliance backbone).

Structured questionnaire for inventorying products, services, and boundaries that fall under the authorization. Feeds the control crosswalk and residual risk decisions.
