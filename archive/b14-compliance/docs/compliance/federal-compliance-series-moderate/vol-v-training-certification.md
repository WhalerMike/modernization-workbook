# Federal Compliance Series - Moderate — Volume V: Training & Certification

**Volume purpose — the enablement layer that teaches Volumes 0–IV and evidences the training slot**

## Draft posture

This document is a draft proposal. It has not been reviewed or approved by the agency CIO Office, the Office of Information Security, or organizational leadership.

## Volume purpose

Volume V is where the series becomes a workforce. Volumes 0–IV describe the naming and identity substrate, the data platform, the security-operations planes, and the governance and authorization machinery. None of that closes a control until someone can build the mechanism, land its evidence, and trace it from the book that specifies it, through the NIST SP 800-53 control it satisfies, to the FedRAMP 20x Key Security Indicator that scores it. Volume V builds that competency — and, in doing so, operationalizes the training program that produces the training-effectiveness record: proof that the people running the program are trained.

The volume is organized as two tracks over the same corpus. A compliance track reads the series as control-closure evidence — one module per evidence slot, book → control → Key Security Indicator → evidence. An implementation track reads it as build instructions — one module per book cluster, each ending by landing an evidence artifact. The volume’s governing claim is that conformance tooling measures drift from an intended state; it cannot author one. A workforce, not a scanner, authors the evidence state — and that competency is itself a control.

## Relationship to Volume IV Book 05

Volume IV Book 05 specifies the awareness and training family program and its control closure. This volume is the academy that operationalizes that program — the two tracks, labs, and certification — and produces the training-effectiveness record. The division is deliberate: Volume IV Book 05 states what the program must be and close; Volume V teaches it, runs the labs, and certifies competency.

## How this volume fits the program

Volume V depends on every architecture volume it teaches. It produces two things the program depends on: trained operators and assessors, and the training-effectiveness record that the authorization package freezes into the structured bundle. Remove Volume V and Volumes I–IV survive as documents, but the path from “the books exist” to “the workforce closes the controls and the training Key Security Indicator evaluates” does not.

## Where evidence lands

The binding of this volume’s closures and evidence to a governance substrate is optional and separable. The volume stands complete at FedRAMP Moderate with no substrate engagement at all. Adopting a substrate adds machine-checked scoring and drift-gating on top of what is already closed, and can be adopted later — or never — without rework.
