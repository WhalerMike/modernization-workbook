# Copilot prompts for the managed service account production proposal

Use these prompts at work, in Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, to fill the proposal's placeholders from the Day 2 evidence, check it for gaps, and draft the change advisory board's summary. The request pack's own prompts, for request rows, are in [../copilot-prompts.md](../copilot-prompts.md).

## Before you use Copilot

- **Use only your agency's approved tools.** Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, signed in with your work account; choose Work if your license shows Work and Web. Never a personal account or a public site.
- **Check what your agency allows.** Your agency's AI-use policy decides what you may paste. Server and account names are agency data: ask your ISSO before you paste them.
- **Never paste a secret.** No password, KDS root key value, token, certificate private key or connection string. The Day 2 script doesn't collect any; don't add one.
- **Copilot drafts. You decide.** Check every value Copilot puts in against the evidence file it came from. A draft isn't evidence, and it isn't an approval.

## 1. Fill the placeholders from the Day 2 evidence

> I'm filling in a managed service account production proposal. Here is its placeholder table, with what each placeholder is and which evidence file fills it: [paste the proposal's section 13 table]. Here is the Day 2 summary.json: [paste summary.json]. And the test results: [paste the T3 and T4 lines]. For each placeholder, give the value from the evidence and the file it came from, as a table. Leave a placeholder blank and list it under "Still to fill" when the evidence doesn't give it. Don't invent a value, a server name or a person.

## 2. Review the proposal for gaps

> Review this proposal for an authorizing official and a change advisory board: [paste the proposal's text]. List, by section: any placeholder still in angle brackets; any statement that a step "satisfies" or "meets" a control (it should say "supports"); any decision asked of the reader that has no owner or date; any prerequisite whose status isn't filled; any risk without a guard; and anything that contradicts another section. Don't rewrite the proposal; list the gaps with the section and the sentence.

## 3. Draft the change advisory board's summary

> Draft a one-page summary for the change advisory board from this proposal: [paste sections 1, 5, 7, 9 and 12]. Use these headings: The change; Why; Scope of the first change (the one-time setup and the pilot); Risk and backout; What we ask the board to approve. Keep it under 400 words, in plain language. Keep every number and decision ID as the proposal states it. Don't add facts the proposal doesn't contain.

## 4. Explain a Day 2 result

> The Day 2 read-only script printed this line: [paste the line]. The step reads [paste the step's row from the script's table]. Explain what the result means for the managed service account proposal, and which placeholder it fills, using only what I pasted.
